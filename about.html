About

I am a lyricist.

My music begins with words.

Je’m is born where Michiko meets Jem.

Jem is an AI vocal persona — a mask.

Michiko writes the lyrics.

Together, we become Je’m.

A collection of words and sounds born from curiosity and experimentation. Each piece begins with lyrics. Through the fusion of human-crafted words with ever-evolving vocal personas and soundscapes, it expands into a unique world of its own.
